# 💫 About Me:

👋 Hi, I'm Parmar Dhaval

🔧 I work primarily in **C++ and Rust**, across whatever kind of development the problem calls for.

🌱 Currently building **real-time projects in C++ and Rust** to sharpen my logic for real-world, low-level programming.

🛠️ I care about creating **developer-friendly tools** — the kind that make other programmers' lives easier.

⚡ I always prefer **performance and safety in all software**. From a very simple app to a large-scale application, I will always choose a **non-GC language**. I don't care about complexity when it buys me safety and performance.

💬 As programmers, we're not supposed to hate any language — but honestly, I really dislike **Java and Python**, and I avoid **JS and web stuff** whenever possible.

🚫 I genuinely dislike **garbage-collected languages — even Go**. No matter how low the overhead, they still can't compete with low-level compiled languages. A GC language, even when compiled, isn't the same as a truly low-level compiled language.

💖 I love **Rust** for making systems-level development *right* — not too much complexity, but with **safety guarantees** that mean far less debugging time. The tooling just works: no CMake, an easy package manager, and **sane defaults** everywhere.

🤝 And I deeply respect **C++** — even with all its problems, people managed the language and used it brilliantly. There was simply **no alternative like Rust back then**, and C++ carried an entire era of systems programming.
